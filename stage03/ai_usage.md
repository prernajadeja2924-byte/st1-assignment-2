# Stage 3 AI Usage Record

## How AI Was Used

AI was used as a support tool during the SmartCare domain modelling activities.

AI helped with:
- Reviewing candidate classes identified from the requirements.
- Reviewing responsibilities and collaborators for the CRC cards.
- Suggesting possible relationships and multiplicities between classes.
- Reviewing the UML design for consistency.
- Suggesting simple Python class skeletons.
- Checking consistency between the UML model and Python class structure.
- Organising and improving the clarity of the written documentation.

## Verification

I reviewed the AI suggestions against the SmartCare requirements before accepting them.

Not every AI suggestion was accepted. Suggestions that introduced unsupported concepts or unnecessary complexity were rejected or modified. For example, NotificationManager and ScheduleEngine were not included because there was not enough evidence for them in the confirmed requirements.

The final design uses Patient, Practitioner and Appointment as the main classes. The Python class skeletons were also executed to check that there were no syntax errors.

## Responsibility

AI was used to support the design process, but I reviewed the suggestions, made the final design decisions and verified that the model remained consistent with the SmartCare requirements.
