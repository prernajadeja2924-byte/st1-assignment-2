# Stage 2 Reflection

During Stage 2, I developed requirements for the SmartCare system based on the client brief. Before using AI, I identified the main stakeholders, functional and non-functional requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria.

AI helped me review the requirements for ambiguity, missing clarification questions and testability. It helped me notice that some requirements needed clearer conditions before they could be properly tested. However, AI could also suggest features that were not supported by the client brief, such as SMS reminders, online payments, facial-recognition login and AI treatment recommendations. These should not automatically become requirements.

One requirement that changed after review was retaining cancelled appointments in the appointment history. The client brief mentions limited appointment history, but it does not specifically confirm how cancelled appointments should be retained. Therefore, I treated this requirement as provisional and requiring client confirmation.

I verified the AI suggestions by comparing them with the SmartCare client brief and the requirements already identified. This showed me that requirements need evidence because unsupported assumptions can increase the project scope or result in software that does not meet the client's actual needs.
